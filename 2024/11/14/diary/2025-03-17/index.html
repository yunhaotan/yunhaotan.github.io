中文自我介绍

尊敬的各位老师，大家好！我是来自华中科技大学土木与水利工程学院智能建造专业的考生谭斌。今天非常荣幸能够有机会在这里向各位老师介绍自己。
在我的大学生活中，有幸参与了《智能化钢筋绑扎机器人》的项目，这是一段宝贵的学习经历。《智能化钢筋绑扎机器人》这个项目在第三届全国智慧城市与智能建造大学生创新创业竞赛中获得了最佳机器人奖和一等奖。此外，我们团队凭借该项目在中美创客大赛武汉赛区获得三等奖的好成绩。值得一提的是，《智能化钢筋绑扎机器人》还成功申请到了专利。
此外，我还很荣幸地获得了华中科技大学2022年的科技创新奖学金以及2022年的自强奖学金。在个人生活方面，我喜欢听歌、宅在家里。音乐对我来说是放松心情、激发灵感的重要源泉。
我希望能够在研究生阶段继续深化专业知识，拓宽研究视野。
感谢各位老师的聆听，谢谢！

英文自我介绍

Respected professors, good morning! I am Tan Bin, a candidate from the Smart Construction program at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. It is my great honor to have this opportunity to introduce myself to you.
During my undergraduate studies, I participated in the Intelligent Steel Reinforcement Binding Robot project, which has been an invaluable learning experience. This project won both the Best Robot Award and First Prize at the National Competition Additionally, our team received the Third Prize in the Wuhan Division of the China-US Young Maker Competition. Notably, we successfully applied for a patent for this innovation.
Furthermore, I was honored to receive both the 2022 Science and Technology Innovation Scholarship and the 2022 Self-Improvement Scholarship from HUST. Outside of academics, I enjoy listening to music and spending time at home. Music serves as both a source of relaxation and creative inspiration for me.
Looking ahead, I aspire to deepen my professional expertise and broaden my research horizons during graduate studies.
Thank you for your attention!
